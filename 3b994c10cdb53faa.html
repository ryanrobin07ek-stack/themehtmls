QUESTIONS & ANSWERS

Frequently Asked Questions

Where exactly are you located in Nairobi CBD?

We are located on Kimathi Street, directly across from the junction with Mama Ngina Street, near Norwich Union House and just a 2-minute stroll from Kencom and Hilton Plaza.

How fast is lunch delivery to CBD office buildings?

For orders within the CBD grid (Lonrho House, View Park, Teleposta, Times Tower, ICEA, etc.), typical delivery time is 15 to 25 minutes from the moment your order is accepted.

Can I pay with M-Pesa on delivery or online?

Yes! We support M-Pesa STK push for seamless checkout, as well as pay-on-pickup and cash on desk delivery.

Do you cater for group meetings and team lunches?

Absolutely. We pack sharing platters, samosa trays, and customized boxed meals for office boardrooms and training sessions. Call or WhatsApp our kitchen 1 hour in advance for groups of 10+.
