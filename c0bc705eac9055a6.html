Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Find Quick Answers To The Most Common Questions About Our Jewellery, Services, And Policies. We're Here To Make Your Experience Simple, Clear, And Worry-Free.

1. What Makes Your Jewellery Unique? +
Every Aviya piece is engineered in-house using ethically sourced, GIA-certified conflict-free diamonds paired with 100% recycled 18k gold and 950 platinum. Our master jewelers hand-set every pavé stone under high-magnification microscopes.
2. Do You Provide Worldwide Shipping? −
Yes, we provide complimentary fully insured express courier shipping to over 85 countries worldwide via armored transit partners. All shipments require an adult signature upon receipt and come packaged in discreet, tamper-evident security boxes.
3. Can I Customize My Piece of Jewellery? +
Absolutely. Our bespoke design salon allows you to select diamond cut, carat weight, metal type, finger size, and complimentary laser engraving of meaningful dates, initials, or coordinates.
4. How Should I Care For Diamond Jewellery? +
We recommend gentle cleaning with warm water, mild ammonia-free dish soap, and a soft-bristled brush every fortnight. Every Aviya purchase includes our complimentary annual prong inspection and ultrasonic spa polish service for life.

Necklace Length & Placement Guide

Understand how different chain lengths rest across collarbones and decolletage.

14" – 16" Collar / Choker

Rests snugly at the base of the throat. Ideal for crew necklines, boatnecks, and layered solitaire pendants.

18" Princess (Universal Standard)

Rests gracefully on the collarbone. The most versatile length for both low-cut cocktail dresses and high-buttoned collars.

20" – 24" Matinee

Falls just below the collarbone to the center of the bustline. Perfect for medallion pendants and plunging necklines.

30" – 36" Opera & Rope

Rests near the breastbone and can be looped twice for a layered choker effect or worn as a dramatic single strand.

Precious Materials & Care

Preserve the eternal brilliance of your diamond and fine gold heirlooms.

18k Gold Alloy Standards

We formulate our gold with 75% pure 24k bullion alloyed with copper and silver for durability. It never discolors, tarnishes, or provokes skin sensitivities.

Routine Cleaning Instructions

Submerge diamonds in lukewarm water mixed with mild dish soap for 15 minutes. Gently brush beneath the pavilion prongs with an ultra-soft cosmetic brush, then dry with a microfiber lint-free cloth.

Lifetime ultrasonic cleaning is complimentary at all Aviya flagship boutiques.

The Gemstone & 4Cs Guide

A collector's guide to Cut, Color, Clarity, and Carat weight.

1. Cut (The Sparkle Factor)

A diamond's cut determines how light enters, reflects, and disperses fire. Aviya exclusively selects Excellent to Ideal cut proportions.

2. Clarity (Purity)

From FL (Flawless) down to VS1 (Very Slightly Included), our stones are strictly eye-clean with zero dark inclusions under the table facet.

3. Color (Rarity)

We curate diamonds in the D to F colorless spectrum to ensure completely icy white reflections in platinum and white gold.

4. Carat (Weight & Spread)

One metric carat equals 200 milligrams. Our shallow pavilion ratios maximize face-up visual diameter per carat point.

The Art of Micro-Pavé Prong Setting

Published by Master Goldsmith Laurent V. • Geneva Atelier

Micro-pavé is not merely a setting technique; it is a microscopic sculpture of precious metal. Unlike traditional bead setting, our artisans work under 40x binocular Leica microscopes to raise minute beads of 18k solid gold that capture adjacent melee diamonds without perceptible metal borders.

Every stone must sit perfectly level, with table facets parallel to within 0.5 degrees. This creates the optical illusion of a continuous diamond river—catching ambient candlelight from across a ballroom.

Founded on the principle that true luxury is quiet, deliberate, and permanent, Maison Aviya operates independent ateliers in Geneva and London. We work exclusively with certified conflict-free diamonds and 100% recycled 18k solid gold.

Each design begins with architectural sketches before our gemologists select matching stones by hand. We believe jewellery should feel weightless to the wearer yet carry an unmistakable presence of distinction.

Frequently Asked Questions

Comprehensive details on diamond certification, bespoke orders, and sizing.

Are Aviya diamonds certified?−
Yes. Every solitaire stone of 0.50 carats and above is graded and laser-inscribed by the Gemological Institute of America (GIA).
Can you engrave custom messages?+
Yes, complimentary laser engraving of up to 24 characters is available on all rings, pendants, and bangles.
What if I order the incorrect ring size?+
We provide one complimentary resizing service within the first 60 days of acquisition, including two-way armored courier shipping.
How does Paystack checkout work?+
Paystack provides bank-grade SSL and 3D Secure verification for international debit and credit cards with instant transaction confirmations.

Have a specific inquiry? Our diamond specialists are available daily.
