The Fire & Sourcing

Rooted in Fire, Elevated by the Tide

At MIKAA, charcoal is not merely fuel—it is our primary seasoning. We burn seasoned wild olive and sustainably coppiced acacia, producing a clean, fragrant heat that seals the pristine natural sweetness of our seafood.

Our seafood arrives each morning on direct ice runs from artisanal fishers in Kilifi Creek and Shimoni Reefs. We believe that true luxury lies in absolute ingredient honesty and culinary respect.

Heritage & Woodcraft

The Philosophy of MIKAA

"Mikaa" is the Swahili word for charcoal embers. We strip away the superfluity of modern fine dining to return to the primal intimacy of open fire.

Part I

An Elemental Way of Eating

Our founders envisioned a restaurant that honors the maritime history of the East African coast. For centuries, dhow trading routes connected Mombasa, Malindi, and Zanzibar with India and the Arabian Peninsula, exchanging spices, techniques, and stories.

At MIKAA, we honor this melting pot through indigenous produce: fermented tamarind, Zanzibar cloves, Kilifi rock salt, cold-pressed coconut oils, and line-caught snapper roasted on hand-forged steel grates.

Part II

Zero-Waste Fire & Ocean Stewardship

We operate a responsible culinary model. We purchase only bycatch and day-boat landings from artisanal fishers who employ sustainable hook-and-line techniques, preserving the coral reef systems.

Our wood is sourced strictly from certified conservation farms managing overgrown wild olive and pruned acacia—never old-growth forest timber. The resulting ash from our hearths is gifted to organic community vegetable growers in Kilifi to enrich soils.

Dining & Reservation Policies

Last updated: October 2026

1. Reservation & Grace Period

We hold reserved tables for 15 minutes past your booking time. If running late, please alert our host team via telephone. Tables may be released to waiting guests after 15 minutes without notification.

2. Dietary Safety & Kitchen Practice

While our kitchen takes rigorous precautions, woodfire smoke and shared grills mean we cannot guarantee 100% absence of airborne allergen traces. Guests with severe airborne sensitivities are asked to inform management prior to arrival.

3. Corkage & Outside Cakes

Our corkage policy allows up to two 750ml bottles of non-list wine per party of four at KSh 2,500 per bottle. Outside cakes for celebrations incur a dessert service fee of KSh 1,500.

4. Payment Security & Data Privacy

Guest contact details collected for reservations are stored exclusively for verification and dining reminders. We never sell, transfer, or distribute customer details to advertising partners.
